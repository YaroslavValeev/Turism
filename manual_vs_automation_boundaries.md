# Manual vs Automation Boundaries

## Keep manual at start
- organizer verification decision
- verified / trusted approval
- disputed deal confirmation
- refund exception approval
- incident severity classification
- first 10 revenue deal validation

## Automate early
- new lead notifications
- stale lead reminders
- review request after completed
- missing card fields alerts
- weekly dashboard aggregation
- commission reminder after accrual

## Automate later
- commission invoicing flows
- advanced lead scoring
- partner attribution automation
- anomaly detection
