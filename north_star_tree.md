# North Star Tree

## North Star
Количество состоявшихся бронирований у verified organizers

## Driver 1 — Supply quality
- active organizers
- published programs
- cards 100% complete
- verified organizer ratio

## Driver 2 — Qualified demand
- qualified leads
- lead -> contacted conversion
- contacted -> booked conversion
- booked -> completed conversion

## Driver 3 — Trust
- reviews per completed booking
- complaint rate
- refund rate
- trust badge coverage

## Driver 4 — Revenue
- GMV
- accrued commission
- collected commission
- avg commission per completed deal
